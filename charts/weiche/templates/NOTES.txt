Use gatewayClassName: weiche in {{ join ", " (.Values.gatewayNamespaces | default (list .Release.Namespace)) }}.
Each Gateway gets a Service and a proxy Deployment in its own namespace.
Inspect the assigned address and conditions with kubectl describe gateway -n NAMESPACE NAME.

Use platform-controlled namespaces for Gateways. Each proxy reads a Secret containing
its routes and authorized TLS material. The compressed snapshot limit is 1 MiB per Gateway.
Create cross-namespace ReferenceGrants in the namespace of the referenced resource.

Delete Gateways before retiring their namespace from gatewayNamespaces or uninstalling
the chart. Kubernetes then removes their proxy workloads, credentials and Services.
